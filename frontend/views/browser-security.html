GuardBridge — Browser Extension Governance

GuardBridge is the browser extension component of AgentHub. It governs AI interactions at the browser layer, operates independently under its own extension identity, and reports minimal, privacy-safe telemetry back to this control plane. No raw prompt text, raw IP addresses, or raw user-agent strings are ever transmitted or stored.
