## Proof points you can cite (research)

- Large-scale indirect prompt injection evaluations: frontier agents remain vulnerable; published ASR ranges roughly 0.5%–8.5%; concealment matters.  
- Silent egress research: network exfiltration can bypass output safety; allowlists outperform prompt-only defenses.  
- Structural finding: no single defense jointly maximizes trustworthiness, utility, and latency for context-dependent agents.

Always attribute; do not oversell lab ASR as a universal SLO.
