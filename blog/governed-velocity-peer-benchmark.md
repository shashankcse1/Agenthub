# Peer Benchmark Questions — CISO to CISO

**Use:** Diligence calls, peer exchanges, advisor meetings  
**Rule:** Ask for clocks and artifacts. Accept discomfort.

---

## Ten questions

1. What is your median **time-to-revoke** for an agent identity — last drill date?  
2. Can you export **tool allow/deny** for a named agent for the last 24 hours in under an hour?  
3. What percentage of tier-1 inference is **on a control plane** vs app-held keys?  
4. How many **unmanaged prod provider keys** exist this week?  
5. Are dual approvals enforced in the **API** or only in a UI?  
6. What is your **MCP/tool default** — deny or allow?  
7. When did you last red-team **concealment** or **silent egress** (not just jailbreaks)?  
8. Do exceptions **auto-expire**? Show the oldest active exception.  
9. What **maturity level** are you under an all-criteria rule — and what blocks the next level?  
10. What external claim did you **refuse** to make because the scorecard wasn’t ready?  

## Scoring a peer (informal)

| Pattern | Signal |
|---------|--------|
| Answers with vendor logos | Filter Era |
| Answers with clocks + dates | Control Plane Era |
| Cannot name freeze authority | Program is advisory only |
| Proud of agent count | Privilege vanity |
| Defers revenue for DoD | Leader behavior |

## Reciprocity

Bring your own Leader Readiness Score band (not necessarily the number) and one drill date. Do not demand clocks you will not share under NDA.
