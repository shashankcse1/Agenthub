# Employee FAQ — Governed Velocity

**Audience:** Everyone who builds, buys, or uses AI at work  
**Tone:** Direct · **Owner:** Comms + OACP  

---

### Why are we doing this?

AI agents can act — not just draft. That multiplies privilege. We want speed without unpriced incidents.

### What is an Agency Contract?

Six rules before production AI can act: Identity, Mandate, Toolbox, Escalation, Fence, Ledger. Think “privileged contractor agreement.”

### Does this mean I can’t experiment?

Experiment in non-prod freely within platform rules. Production agency needs a Contract and Definition of Done. Shadow prod keys are out.

### Will this slow my roadmap?

It may defer launches that cannot meet the bar. It prevents the longer freeze after a bad agent incident. That is governed velocity.

### What if my agent “just summarizes”?

If it has tools, send, browse, or prod credentials, it is not “just” a summarizer. When in doubt, ask OACP.

### What is the control plane?

The mandatory path for production AI decisions — who acted, under what policy, with which tools — with an audit ledger.

### What should I do this week?

1. Inventory AI paths you own (apps, scripts, vendors)  
2. Remove shared provider keys from prod configs  
3. Book steward office hours if you ship agents

### Bottom line

If it can act, it needs a contract. If it has a contract, it needs a ledger. If it has neither, it is not production.
