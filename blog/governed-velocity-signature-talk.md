# Signature Talk — 18 Minutes

**Title:** The Privilege Multiplier  
**Subtitle:** Why leaders left the Filter Era  
**Use:** Flagship keynote (longer than the 12-minute cut)  
**Prop:** One slide with six clauses · one slide with clocks (15m / 60m)

---

## 0:00 — Cold open

A chatbot that answers badly wastes time.  
An agent that acts badly becomes a company story — sometimes while the UI says success.

Most companies are buying models.  
Leaders are putting **agency under contract**.

## 2:00 — Privilege multiplier

Every tool, key, and data path compounds blast radius.  
Five MCP servers is not integration. It is a temporary executive.

## 5:00 — The wrong question

~~Is the model safe?~~  
**Who is acting, with what power, on what data, under what rule — and where is the proof?**

## 7:00 — Evidence without theater

Indirect injection works on frontier agents. Concealment is the nightmare. Silent egress beats chat filters. No single defense wins trust, utility, and latency.

So we stop hunting silver filters.

## 10:00 — Agency Contract

Identity · Mandate · Toolbox · Escalation · Fence · Ledger  

Teach with the worked example: leadership is what you **remove** (`email.send`, `web.fetch`).

## 13:00 — Clocks

Median revoke ≤ 15 minutes.  
Evidence export ≤ 60 minutes.  
If you cannot say the numbers, you have a blog — not a program.

## 15:00 — Anti-patterns (30 seconds each)

Prompt as policy · UI-only dual approval · L4 by press release · chat as forensic  

## 16:30 — Close

We will not slow AI to feel safe.  
We will not ship autonomy without a control plane.  

Governed velocity: ship at the speed of the business, contain at the speed of an incident, prove at the speed of an audit.

**Who is acting — and where is the proof?**

## Optional final beat

Finance is not funding more tokens.  
Finance is funding a brake, a meter, and a ledger.
