# Governed Velocity — 90-Day Communications Calendar

**Principle:** Same vocabulary every week. No surprise product launches before doctrine lands.

---

## Week −1 (pre-declare)

| Audience | Message | Asset |
|----------|---------|-------|
| CEO / CISO / CTO | Align on thesis + maturity honesty | exec brief · maturity model |
| Risk committee chair | Preview resolution | board-resolution · board one-pager |
| Comms | Embargo external until day 7+ | analyst brief (draft) |

---

## Days 1–7 — Declare

| Day | Audience | Channel | Asset |
|-----|----------|---------|-------|
| 1 | Company | Email / all-hands | CEO letter or internal-email |
| 2 | Engineering | Slack/office hours | CTO one-pager |
| 3 | Security + GRC | Working session | policy AI-CTRL-001 |
| 5 | App owners | Invite to workshop | workshop.md |
| 7 | Sponsors | Week-1 readout | scorecard (day 0 baselines) |

**External:** silent unless board requires disclosure.

---

## Days 8–30 — Launch

| Week | Internal | External |
|------|----------|----------|
| 2 | Inventory amnesty stories (blame-free) | — |
| 3 | Workshop readouts; first on-plane win | — |
| 4 | Day-30 scorecard to execs | Optional: LinkedIn cold open from manifesto |

---

## Days 31–60 — Constrain

| Cadence | Message |
|---------|---------|
| Biweekly | Exception register transparency (counts, not shame) |
| Monthly eng forum | Definition of Done gate examples (pass/fail anonymized) |
| Day 60 | Exec scorecard + maturity re-assess |

**External optional:** Publish manifesto; keynote abstract to events. Still doctrine-first.

---

## Days 61–90 — Prove

| Day | Audience | Message |
|-----|----------|---------|
| ~70 | Security | Red-team RT-01 + RT-02 completed |
| ~80 | Compliance | Evidence export RTO number |
| 90 | Board / risk | Residual risk page + resolution reporting items |
| 90 | Company | “What changed” note: coverage, revoke time, open findings |

**GTM:** Only after day-60 doctrine credibility — use GTM talk track; product doctrine last.

---

## Standing messages (do not drift)

1. Privilege multiplier  
2. Filter Era → Control Plane Era  
3. Agency Contract (six clauses)  
4. Outcome integrity &gt; refusal rate  
5. Stopwatch metrics (revoke, export)  
6. Honest maturity — no L4 theater  

## Forbidden messages

- “We solved prompt injection”  
- “Zero risk AI”  
- Feature tours before Contract language  
- Maturity claims without scorecard
