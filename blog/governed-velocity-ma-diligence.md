# M&A Diligence — AI Agency Addendum

**Use:** Acquiring a company / product with agents, copilots, MCP, or embedded AI actions  
**Standard:** Governed Velocity  
**Rule:** Model demos are not diligence. Privilege graphs are.

---

## 1. Estate discovery

1. Inventory all production agents and AI paths (apps, scripts, browser, vendors).  
2. List every provider key / NHI with prod scope — age, owner, shared?  
3. Map tool/MCP graph per agent (including transitive).  
4. Identify fail-open paths (bypass gateways, debug flags, “admin mode”).  

## 2. Contract gap assessment

Score each material agent against six clauses (R/A/G).  
Any prod agent lacking Identity or Ledger = **material finding**.

## 3. Evidence & incidents

| Ask | Evidence |
|-----|----------|
| Last 12 months AI/agent incidents | Tickets + postmortems |
| Can they export tool allow/deny for 24h? | Timed drill in diligence |
| Red team for IPI / egress? | Reports + open findings |
| Training data / retention commitments | Contracts + configs |

## 4. Financial & legal hooks

- Unlimited liability paths via agent actions (payments, delete, email storms)  
- Customer commitments about “human in the loop” vs actual automation  
- Cyber insurance questionnaire answers vs reality  
- Regulatory exposure (sector + geography)

## 6. Deal implications (examples)

| Finding | Implication |
|---------|-------------|
| Shared god keys in prod | Price chip + close condition: keys rotated pre-close |
| No ledger for tool calls | Integration cost + delayed customer expansion |
| Fail-open on policy error | Hard control debt; may defer AI revenue recognition assumptions |
| Strong Contract + drills | Accelerant; lower integration risk |

## 7. Diligence disposition

- [ ] Proceed  
- [ ] Proceed with control-debt escrow / holdback  
- [ ] Defer AI product lines from day-1 operation  
- [ ] Walk  

**Sign-off:** CISO · CTO · Deal lead · (CFO if material holdback)
