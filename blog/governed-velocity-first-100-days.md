# First 100 Days — Head of AI Control Plane

**Role:** Make governed velocity real. You are not the prompt team. You are the chokepoint owner.  
**North star:** On-plane coverage, revoke clock, export clock, zero unmanaged keys, honest maturity.

---

## Days 1–14 — See without theater

| Day | Action | Done when |
|-----|--------|-----------|
| 1–2 | Read Policy AI-CTRL-001, appetite, RACI, SLOs | You can recite six clauses + refuse list |
| 3–5 | Inventory AI paths + keys with Security | Draft estate map exists |
| 5–7 | Baseline scorecard (even if ugly) | Day-0 numbers written; empty ≠ green |
| 8–10 | Meet top 5 app owners; no demos, only privilege graphs | Toolbox lists captured |
| 11–14 | Declare maturity honestly (likely L0–L2) | Sponsors heard the number without spin |

**Personal rule:** If someone says “we’re basically secure,” ask for revoke median.

---

## Days 15–45 — Chokepoint and freeze credibility

1. Ship / harden paved road (Epic A) — official path for new prod inference  
2. Kill or schedule-kill unmanaged prod keys  
3. Run first revoke drill; publish the minutes  
4. Establish exception register with auto-expiry  
5. Exercise freeze once on a real non-compliant launch (painful = real)  

**Credibility test:** An SVP cannot bypass you with a slide.

---

## Days 46–75 — Contract at scale

1. Steward curriculum cohort #1  
2. Day-zero onboarding mandatory for new agents  
3. Epics B+C (mandate + toolbox) on tier-1  
4. Dual-approval enforcement verified via API (not UI)  
5. Vendor questionnaire on top AI suppliers  
6. RT-01 + RT-02 executed; findings owned  

---

## Days 76–100 — Prove and institutionalize

1. Evidence export drill ≤ 60m  
2. OACP QBR #1 with numbers-first packet  
3. Day-90-style board extract (even if calendar ≠ day 90)  
4. Crisis holding statement reviewed with Comms  
5. Anti-pattern review: kill one piece of theater publicly inside eng  
6. Propose year budget using CFO brief language  

**Exit interview with yourself:** Can you answer — who is acting, with what power, on what data, under what rule, and where is the proof — for every tier-1 agent?

---

## Standing weekly cadence

| Meeting | Length | Only agenda |
|---------|--------|-------------|
| Coverage standup | 25m | On-plane %, keys, blockers |
| Exception scrub | 20m biweekly | Ageing, expiry, auto-disable |
| Steward office hours | 45m | Contracts, DoD, removals |

## Do not do in the first 100 days

- Rewrite the manifesto for fun  
- Model bake-offs  
- Promise L4  
- Accept permanent exceptions  
- Trade fail-closed for “just this launch”  

**Close:** Your product is not AI. Your product is **bounded privilege with a ledger.**
