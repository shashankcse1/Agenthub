## Customer brief (copy)

### How we govern AI agency

We operate production AI under **Governed Velocity**: ship fast, contain privilege, prove decisions.

Every production agent runs under an **Agency Contract**:

| Clause | What it means for you |
|--------|------------------------|
| Identity | Named, scoped, revocable non-human identities — not shared god keys |
| Mandate | Your content is treated as data; policies bound what can be sent/used |
| Toolbox | Tools/MCP are allowlisted; least agency by default |
| Escalation | Irreversible acts require human control paths |
| Fence | Budgets and egress controls bound blast radius |
| Ledger | Material allow/deny decisions are auditable and exportable |

We do **not** claim prompt injection is solved. We assume models can be fooled and still require mediation, least agency, and evidence.

### What we can demonstrate

- Control-plane mediation for production inference/tool paths  
- Revoke and evidence-export drills against numeric targets  
- Vendor review for third-party AI components we enable  
- Time-bounded exceptions only — no permanent silent privilege  

### What we ask of customer environments

When our agents act inside *your* tenancy, shared responsibility applies: your identity provider, your data classification, your approval of tool scopes. We will not recommend bypassing your control plane to “go faster.”
