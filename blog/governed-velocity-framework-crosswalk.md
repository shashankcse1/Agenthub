## EU AI Act — orientation (not legal advice)

| Concern regulators emphasize | Our operational answer |
|------------------------------|------------------------|
| Accountability for AI systems | Named owners · RACI · Contract on file |
| Transparency / traceability | Ledger · evidence export |
| Human oversight for consequential acts | Escalation clause · dual approval |
| Risk management | Maturity · scorecard · residual-risk board page |
| Data governance | Mandate · input data policies |

High-risk classification and legal obligations require counsel. This doctrine improves readiness; it does not replace conformity assessment.

## One-slide auditor narrative

We mediate production AI agency through a control plane under a six-clause Agency Contract. We measure coverage, approval enforcement, revoke time, and evidence export. We red-team concealment and silent egress. We accept residual risk explicitly — we do not claim filters solved injection.
