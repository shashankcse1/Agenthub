# Governed Velocity — 12-minute keynote

**Title:** Governed Velocity: Leaving the Filter Era  
**Audience:** CISO summit / board tech committee / AI platform leaders  
**Tone:** Doctrine, not demo

---

## Slide 1 — Cold open (0:00–0:45)

**On screen:** *Most companies are buying models. Leaders are putting agency under contract.*

**Say:**  
A chatbot that answers badly wastes time. An agent that acts badly becomes a company story. Tonight is not about safer prompts. It is about who is allowed to act — and whether you can prove it.

---

## Slide 2 — The wrong question (0:45–1:45)

**On screen:** ~~Is the model safe?~~ → **Can we control and prove agency?**

**Say:**  
“Is the model safe?” belongs to the Filter Era. Leaders ask the operational question. If you cannot answer it in one breath, you have demos with production credentials.

---

## Slide 3 — Privilege multiplier (1:45–3:00)

**On screen:** *AI multiplies privilege, not just productivity.*

**Say:**  
Every tool, key, and data path compounds blast radius. Five MCP servers is not integration. It is a temporary executive with no pause button — unless you build one.

---

## Slide 4 — Evidence, not vibes (3:00–4:30)

**On screen:**  
- Every frontier agent stack tested: vulnerable  
- ASR ~0.5%–8.5%  
- Concealment: clean answer, dirty side effect  
- Silent egress: network > chat filters

**Say:**  
Cite the research briefly. Then land the line: outcome integrity beats refusal rate.

---

## Slide 5 — Filter Era vs Control Plane Era (4:30–5:30)

**On screen:** Two columns — Filter Era / Control Plane Era

**Say:**  
Filters help. They do not govern. Leaders assume hijack and still require mediation.

---

## Slide 6 — Agency Contract (5:30–7:30)

**On screen:** Six clauses — Identity · Mandate · Toolbox · Escalation · Fence · Ledger

**Say:**  
This is the doctrine. Walk each clause in one sentence. Pause on Ledger: if proof lives in Slack, you are already late.

---

## Slide 7 — Control plane sketch (7:30–8:15)

**On screen:** Simple chokepoint diagram (apps → control plane → models/tools/data/egress)

**Say:**  
One place for identity, policy, tools, budgets, approvals, evidence. Without it, you have a federation of future incidents.

---

## Slide 8 — Five laws (8:15–9:15)

**On screen:** Assume hijack · Mediate before accelerate · Measure side effects · Prove before scale · Tell residual risk plainly

**Say:**  
These are non-negotiables. Skip any one and you are performing security, not practicing it.

---

## Slide 9 — 90 days (9:15–10:15)

**On screen:** See → Constrain → Prove + scoreboard metrics

**Say:**  
Leaders do not announce a three-year AI safety program. They show a stopwatch and a day-90 scoreboard.

---

## Slide 10 — Board paragraph (10:15–10:45)

**On screen:** Full board paragraph (governed velocity)

**Say:**  
Read it slowly. Offer it as language they can take into the next committee meeting unchanged.

---

## Slide 11 — Close (10:45–12:00)

**On screen:** *Who is acting — and where is the proof?*

**Say:**  
Most will keep buying models and hoping filters hold. Leaders will build the control plane, put agency under contract, and move. That is governed velocity.

**Optional Q cue:** “I’ll take the hard questions — starting with ‘doesn’t this slow us down?’ The answer is: unmanaged agency is what slows you down — after the incident.”

---

## Hard Q&A (speaker prep)

| Question | Leader answer |
|----------|----------------|
| Won’t this slow innovation? | Unmediated agency slows you after the breach. Governed velocity is how you keep shipping. |
| Can’t the vendor’s safety layer handle this? | Vendor safety is necessary. It is not your control plane, your identity, or your ledger. |
| Isn’t prompt injection unsolved? | Correct. That is why we design for containment, not for a perfect filter. |
| What’s the first dollar we spend? | Inventory + kill unmanaged keys + force production inference through one chokepoint. |
| How do we know it’s working? | Gateway coverage, dual-approval coverage, egress denies, time-to-revoke, evidence export RTO. |
| What do we tell the board if we’re early? | We are leaving the Filter Era. Here is the Agency Contract and the 90-day prove plan. |
