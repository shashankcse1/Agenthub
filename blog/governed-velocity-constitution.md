# Constitution — Governed Velocity

**Status:** Founding rules · amend only with Board/Risk + CISO + CTO  
**Length:** One page · higher than any playbook  

---

## Article I — Purpose

The enterprise shall pursue AI capability under **governed velocity**: ship at the speed of the business, contain at the speed of an incident, prove at the speed of an audit.

## Article II — Era

The **Filter Era** (hope the model refuses) is insufficient for agency. The enterprise operates in the **Control Plane Era**: mediate agency, contain privilege, keep the ledger.

## Article III — The Question

All production AI agency shall answer:  
**Who is acting, with what power, on what data, under what rule — and where is the proof?**

## Article IV — The Contract

Production agents shall operate under the Agency Contract:  
**Identity · Mandate · Toolbox · Escalation · Fence · Ledger.**

## Article V — Clocks

The enterprise shall maintain measurable capability to revoke agency and export evidence. Targets: **revoke ≤ 15 minutes**, **evidence export ≤ 60 minutes**, unless Risk Committee resets targets upward in stringency only.

## Article VI — Refusals

The enterprise refuses: fail-open tier-1 mediation; unmanaged production provider keys; permanent Contract exceptions; chat as system of record for side effects; external leadership claims beyond the scorecard.

## Article VII — Authority

An Office of the AI Control Plane (or equivalent) shall hold authority to gate production agency and freeze net-new agents when Identity, Escalation, or Ledger governance SLOs are breached without an approved plan.

## Article VIII — Honesty

Maturity and “leader” claims shall follow all-criteria evidence. **We will not narrate past our scorecard.**

## Article IX — Amendment

Amendments require written approval of Risk Committee (or Board), CISO, and CTO, with decision-log ID.
