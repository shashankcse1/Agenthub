## Blackout / trigger overrides

On S1, appetite trigger, or governance SLO Red &gt; 7 days:

- Freeze net-new prod agents  
- Daily coverage war-room until lift criteria met  
- Skip “narrative” work (talks, campaigns) until clocks recover
