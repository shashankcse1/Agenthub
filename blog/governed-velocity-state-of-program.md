# State of the Program — All-Hands Script (12 minutes)

**Cadence:** Quarterly · **Speakers:** CISO + OACP Head  
**Slide count:** ≤ 6  

---

## 0:00 — Open

We run AI under **governed velocity** — not under vibes. Today: numbers, not adjectives.

## 2:00 — The Contract (30 seconds)

Six clauses. Desk card. Chat is not the system of record.

## 3:00 — Scoreboard

On-plane % · unmanaged keys · revoke clock · export clock · exceptions aged · open P1/P2  

Say the reds out loud.

## 6:00 — What we deferred

Name launches stopped by DoD. Thank the stewards. This is leadership, not bureaucracy.

## 8:00 — What broke / what we drilled

One sanitized lesson from tabletop or incident. Clauses that failed. What we removed from a toolbox.

## 10:00 — What’s next

Epic focus · steward cohort · Game Day date · readiness band (not a brag)

## 11:30 — Close

We will not narrate past our scorecard.  
If it can act, it needs a contract. If it has a contract, it needs a ledger.

**Q&A:** 10 minutes · park product feature requests to office hours.
