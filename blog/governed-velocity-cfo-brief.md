# CFO Brief — The Business Case for Governed Velocity

**Audience:** CFO / Finance Committee  
**Ask:** Fund the AI control plane and OACP as risk-reducing infrastructure — not as a discretionary “AI innovation” line  
**Read time:** 4 minutes

---

## The financial framing

AI agents do not only create productivity optionality. They create **privilege optionality** — the ability to act across tools, data, and egress paths at machine speed.

Finance already understands this pattern: shared admin credentials, unmonitored SaaS, and shadow IT were cheap until they were not. Unmediated AI agency is the same balance-sheet shape with a faster clock.

**Governed velocity** is how we keep the upside (throughput) while pricing the downside (blast radius, audit failure, incident freeze).

---

## Cost of inaction (order-of-magnitude)

| Failure mode | Financial / operational impact |
|--------------|--------------------------------|
| Silent exfil via agent tool | Breach response, notify, legal, customer credit, dilution of trust |
| Rogue / overeager prod mutation | Downtime, rollback, revenue deferral, brand |
| Credential sprawl (god keys) | Extended dwell time; harder containment; insurer scrutiny |
| Audit inability to produce evidence | Findings, delayed deals, higher cyber premium, regulatory friction |
| Post-incident freeze on all AI | Opportunity cost: roadmaps stop while competitors under contract keep shipping |

The expensive outcome is rarely the model bill. It is the **unplanned stop** after unpriced privilege.

---

## Investment ask (typical year-1 shape)

| Item | Why finance should recognize it |
|------|----------------------------------|
| Control plane platform capacity | Chokepoint = measurable coverage % |
| OACP (fractional to small team) | Owner of gates, exceptions, drills |
| Red-team + evidence drills | Turns “we think” into timed numbers |
| Vendor agency reviews | Stops buying privilege disguised as SaaS |

Compare to: unbounded inference spend + unbounded incident optionality with no revoke SLA.

---

## KPIs finance can underwrite

| KPI | Why it is a finance KPI |
|-----|-------------------------|
| % tier-1 inference on control plane | Unmediated spend / risk shrinks |
| Unmanaged prod keys | Leading indicator of breach cost |
| Median time-to-revoke | Incident loss function |
| Evidence export RTO | Deal / audit friction cost |
| Exception count & age | Hidden risk inventory |
| AI spend under budget policy | Direct P&L control |

Vanity metric to reject: “refusal rate improved.” It does not bind spend or blast radius.

---

## Return narrative (how to defend the spend)

1. **Avoided freeze** — competitors without a contract will halt AI broadly after their first agent incident; we continue under reduced privilege.  
2. **Insurability & diligence** — exporters of evidence clear customer and insurer questionnaires faster.  
3. **Spend governance** — budgets and anomalies are first-class fences, not monthly surprise.  
4. **Capital efficiency** — prove-before-scale stops funding demos that cannot answer who acted.

---

## Decision

**Approve** multi-quarter funding for control-plane enforcement + OACP minimum staffing, gated on day-30/60/90 scorecard reviews with CISO/CTO.

**Defer** net-new production agent programs that cannot pass Definition of Done — treat them as unbudgeted risk, not innovation.

---

## One line for the finance committee

We are not buying more model calls. We are buying a brake, a meter, and a ledger — so AI throughput remains an asset instead of an unpriced liability.
