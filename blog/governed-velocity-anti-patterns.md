## Sentences that should trigger a stop

1. “The model isn’t supposed to do that.”  
2. “We’ll add logging after GA.”  
3. “Security approved the design” (no DoD artifact).  
4. “It’s just a summarizer” (but it has send/tools).  
5. “Shared key is fine for the team.”  
6. “We can’t mediate — latency.” (no measurement of mediation overhead)  
7. “Board doesn’t need metrics, just assurance.”

## Recovery move (when theater is found)

1. Tell the truth on maturity (likely L0–L1).  
2. Freeze net-new prod agency.  
3. Inventory + key kill.  
4. Re-enter leader path at Declare — not at Narrate.  

**Leader line:** We will not narrate past our scorecard.
