# Internal Launch Email / All-Hands Script

**From:** CISO + CTO (joint)  
**To:** Company / engineering + product + security  
**Subject:** We’re leaving the Filter Era — Agency Contract now in effect for production AI

---

## Email body

Team —

We are changing how we ship AI.

For the last period, too much of the industry — and too much of our own muscle memory — treated AI security as a filter problem: safer prompts, safer models, better refusals. That is the **Filter Era**. It is not enough for agents that can call tools, touch data, and act with real privilege.

AI is a **privilege multiplier**. A bad chat answer wastes time. A bad agent action becomes an incident.

Effective [DATE], production AI agents operate under **governed velocity**:

> Ship at the speed of the business. Contain at the speed of an incident. Prove at the speed of an audit.

That means an **Agency Contract** before production agency:

1. **Identity** — scoped, owned, short-lived keys  
2. **Mandate** — untrusted context cannot silently rewrite goals  
3. **Toolbox** — approved tools/MCP only; least agency  
4. **Escalation** — humans approve irreversible / production-impacting acts  
5. **Fence** — budgets, egress controls, kill switch  
6. **Ledger** — evidence we can export  

### What happens in the next 30 days

- Inventory every AI path (apps, scripts, browser, vendors) — amnesty for honest reporting  
- Unmanaged production keys get owners and revocation dates  
- New production inference goes through the control plane  
- We will measure coverage, revoke time, and evidence export — not vanity refusal rates  

If you own an agent or an integration, you will be asked to put it under contract. Platform and Security will help. Permanent exceptions are how privilege multiplies quietly — we will not do that.

We are not slowing AI down. We are refusing to ship unpriced privilege.

Details: [link to exec brief / Contract policy]  
Office hours: [time]

— [CISO], [CTO]
