# Charter — Office of the AI Control Plane (OACP)

**Status:** Recommended standing function  
**Reports to:** CTO (delivery) with dotted line to CISO (standard)  
**Mission:** Make governed velocity real — mediate production agency, enforce the Agency Contract, keep the ledger honest.

---

## 1. Why this exists

AI agency crosses app teams, security, data, and vendors. Without a standing owner, the Control Plane Era collapses back into Filter Era habits: prompts, hope, and Slack evidence.

The OACP is the program office for the chokepoint.

## 2. Scope

**In**

- Control-plane platform defaults and coverage metrics  
- Agency Contract stewardship and Definition of Done gate  
- MCP / tool allowlist operations  
- Scorecard integrity and prove drills (revoke, evidence export)  
- Vendor agency questionnaire for AI products  
- Exception register administration  
- Red-team scenario backlog with Security  

**Out**

- Model research / foundation model training  
- Generic AppSec of non-AI apps (partner with AppSec)  
- Business prioritization of AI use cases (partner with product)  

## 3. Authority

OACP may:

1. Block production go-live lacking Definition of Done  
2. Revoke or reduce privileges on expiry of exceptions  
3. Require on-plane routing for tier-1 inference  
4. Freeze new prod agents when Identity / Escalation / Ledger scorecard is Red  

Escalation: CTO (engineering conflict) · CISO (risk acceptance) · Board risk (residual risk / S1).

## 4. Staffing (minimum viable)

| Role | FTE (indicative) | Focus |
|------|------------------|--------|
| Head of AI Control Plane | 0.5–1.0 | A for platform enforcement |
| Control plane engineer(s) | 1–3 | Gateway, policy, MCP, budgets |
| AI security engineer | 0.5–1.0 | Red team, incident, vendor review |
| Governance analyst | 0.5 | Scorecard, exceptions, evidence drills |

Small orgs: combine roles; do not combine **risk acceptance (CISO)** with **build pressure (app owner)**.

## 5. Cadence

| Meeting | Cadence | Outcome |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Coverage standup | Weekly | On-plane %, blockers |
| Exception review | Biweekly | Approve / deny / expire |
| Scorecard exec | 30 / 60 / 90 · quarterly | Honest status |
| Tabletop | Quarterly | Time-to-revoke, export RTO |
| Policy review | Quarterly | AI-CTRL-001 updates |

## 6. Success definition (year 1)

- Tier-1 production inference on-plane at target  
- Unmanaged prod keys = 0  
- Exceptions all time-bounded; register clean  
- Revoke and evidence drills routine  
- Maturity ≥ L3 for tier-1 estate  
- Board residual-risk narrative delivered without scramble  

## 7. Anti-patterns

- OACP becomes a prompt-engineering guild  
- OACP becomes a ticket sink with no freeze authority  
- OACP reports only to GTM or only to a single app team  
- Maturity claimed at L4 without scorecard evidence
