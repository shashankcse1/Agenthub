# Governed Velocity — GTM Talk Track (Doctrine First)

**Rule:** 8 minutes of doctrine before any product screen. If the buyer only wants features, still open with the era shift — then operationalize.

---

## 0:00–1:00 — Cold open

Most companies are buying models. Leaders are putting agency under contract.  
AI is a privilege multiplier. Chatbots waste time when wrong; agents create company stories.

**Question to buyer:** Are your production agents under contract — or just under a prompt?

---

## 1:00–3:00 — Era shift

| Filter Era | Control Plane Era |
|------------|-------------------|
| Is the model safe? | Can we control and prove agency? |
| Refusal rate | Outcome integrity + time-to-revoke |
| Keys in every app | One chokepoint |

Land: prompt injection is not solved. That is why filters alone fail. Leaders design for containment.

---

## 3:00–5:30 — Agency Contract

Walk six clauses in one sentence each.  
Ask after Toolbox: “How many MCP servers can your agents reach today — and who approved them?”  
Ask after Ledger: “If the board asked Friday for evidence of who approved last week’s tool call — how long would that take?”

---

## 5:30–7:30 — Operationalize (product)

Only now: map clauses → control plane capabilities (use `governed-velocity-guardbridge.md`).  
Show one path: identity → policy → tool mediation → dual approval → audit export.  
Do not tour every console.

**Procurement line:**  
Does this put your agents under contract — with a ledger — or hand you another key?

---

## 7:30–8:30 — Close + next step

Propose day-30 style pilot outcomes, not a feature POC:

1. One tier-1 path on-plane with audit  
2. MCP allowlist v0  
3. Timed evidence export  
4. Timed revoke  

**Close:** Governed velocity — ship fast, contain privilege, keep the proof.

---

## Objection handling (short)

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| We already have a model gateway | Gateway for tokens ≠ contract for agency. Tools, approvals, egress, ledger? |
| Our model vendor is safe | Necessary ≠ sufficient. You still own identity and proof. |
| This will slow delivery | Unmediated agency slows you after the incident. |
| Just show the UI | Happy to — after we agree what “good” means. |
