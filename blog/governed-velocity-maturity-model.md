# Governed Velocity — Maturity Model (L0–L4)

**Use:** Honest self-assessment for boards and platform roadmaps.  
**Rule:** You are the level where **all** criteria are met — not the level where some projects shine.

---

## Overview

| Level | Name | One-line |
|-------|------|----------|
| L0 | Unpriced privilege | Agents and keys exist; nobody can inventory them |
| L1 | Filter Era | Safety prompts and vendor controls; no agency contract |
| L2 | Chokepoint | Production inference mediated; identity hygiene started |
| L3 | Under contract | Six clauses enforced on tier-1; scorecard live |
| L4 | Governed velocity | Prove-before-scale is culture; residual risk is explicit |

Leaders do not claim L4 in a press release. They show the scorecard.

---

## L0 — Unpriced privilege

**Signals**

- Unknown AI paths (apps, scripts, browser, vendors)  
- Shared production API keys  
- No owner for agent identities  
- Incidents would start with “who deployed that?”  

**Exit to L1:** Inventory started; executive sponsor named; vocabulary introduced.

---

## L1 — Filter Era

**Signals**

- System prompts / vendor safety enabled  
- Some DLP or content filters  
- Still asking primarily “is the model safe?”  
- Tools/MCP connected opportunistically  
- Evidence in tickets and Slack  

**Exit to L2:** Control plane designated; new production inference forbidden off-plane; unmanaged keys have owners and kill dates.

---

## L2 — Chokepoint

**Signals**

- Majority of tier-1 inference on control plane  
- NHI inventory exists  
- Basic audit on gated paths  
- Budgets or rate limits on top spenders  
- Agency Contract drafted as policy (may not be fully enforced)  

**Exit to L3:** Policy adopted (signatures); tier-1 routes have input/output policy; MCP allowlist enforced; dual approval on scoped prod mutations; scorecard reporting.

---

## L3 — Under contract

**Signals**

- AI-CTRL-001 (or equivalent) adopted  
- Six clauses met for tier-1 agents  
- Day-30/60/90 scorecard reviewed by CISO  
- Timed revoke and evidence export exist  
- Exceptions time-bounded  

**Exit to L4:** Concealment + silent-egress red teams recurring; prove-before-scale gate on all new prod agents; board residual-risk narrative habitual; permanent exception list empty.

---

## L4 — Governed velocity

**Signals**

- Control-plane coverage ≥ target with trend discipline  
- Outcome integrity metrics outrank toxicity vanity metrics  
- Kill-switch and ledger drills are boring (routine)  
- Product reviews reject off-contract agency automatically  
- External story matches internal scoreboard  

**Stay honest:** A single hero agent at L4 does not make the enterprise L4.
