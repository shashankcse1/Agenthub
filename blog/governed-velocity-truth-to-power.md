# Speaking Truth to Power — Scripts for CISOs & OACP Heads

**Use when:** Executives want speed without contract, or narrative without clocks.  
**Tone:** Calm, numeric, loyal to the company — not to the demo.

---

## When told “Just ship it; we’ll secure it later”

I can ship a demo tomorrow. I will not ship production agency without Identity, Toolbox, and Ledger — because “later” is how we buy an unpriced incident. The Definition of Done is the release gate. If we override it, I need a time-bounded exception with auto-disable and your name on the risk acceptance — not a Slack thumbs-up.

## When told “The model vendor already handles safety”

Vendor safety is necessary and insufficient. They do not own our NHI, our MCP graph, our dual-approval, or our evidence export. If the board asks who approved last week’s tool call, “the model refused sometimes” is not an answer.

## When told “This will slow innovation”

Unmanaged agency slows us after the freeze. Governed velocity is how we keep shipping when competitors halt. Show me the mediation latency SLO and the revenue feature blocked by DoD — then we optimize the paved road. We do not optimize by removing the brake.

## When told “Say we’re industry-leading in the keynote”

Our Leader Readiness Score is __ / 40. Below 32 we do not use leader language. We can tell a honest chokepoint story. I will not narrate past our scorecard — that becomes a credibility incident later.

## When told “Fail open if the gateway blips”

That violates risk appetite and architecture principle P2. Tier-1 fail-open is how silent privilege returns during the exact window attackers love. We recover availability deliberately; we do not disable governance to clear a dashboard.

## When told “Permanent exception for this strategic customer”

Permanent exceptions are prohibited. I can offer ≤ 90 days with compensating controls and auto-disable. Strategic does not mean unpriced. If the customer requires unbounded agency, that is a deal-structure conversation with Risk — not a silent toolbox expansion.

## Closing line (universal)

I am not here to stop AI. I am here to make sure AI’s privilege stays a company asset — with a ledger we can defend.
