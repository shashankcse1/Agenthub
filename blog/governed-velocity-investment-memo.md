# Investment Committee Memo — AI Control Plane Program

**To:** Investment / Operating Committee  
**From:** CTO + CISO  
**Re:** Multi-quarter funding for Governed Velocity (control plane + OACP)  
**Ask:** Approve program budget and freeze authority as risk infrastructure  

---

## Recommendation

**Approve** funding for the AI control plane paved road, OACP minimum staffing, and prove cadence (drills, red team, instrumentation).  

**Classify** spend as **risk-reducing infrastructure** with productivity optionality — not as discretionary “AI innovation theater.”

## Problem

AI agents multiply privilege. Unmediated agency creates unpriced liability: incident freeze, audit failure, insurer friction, and deferred roadmaps after the first serious event. Filter Era controls (prompts, vendor safety marketing) do not bind side effects.

## Solution

Governed Velocity: Agency Contract on a mediated control plane; numeric revoke/export SLOs; prove-before-scale gates; honest maturity.
