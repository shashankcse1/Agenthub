# Control Attestation Letter — Template

**Use:** Auditors, insurers, enterprise customers under NDA  
**Signatory:** CISO (or delegate with written authority)  
**Rule:** Attest only to controls you can evidence from the binder  

---

[Date]  

To: [Recipient]  

**Re: Attestation of AI agency governance controls — Governed Velocity**

We operate production AI systems capable of tool use and other side effects under an internal standard known as **Governed Velocity**. Production agents are required to operate under an **Agency Contract** (identity, mandate, toolbox, escalation, fence, ledger) mediated by an AI **control plane**.

Without limitation, we maintain:

1. **Mediation** — Production inference and tool invocation for in-scope systems are intended to traverse the control plane; unmanaged shared production provider keys are prohibited.  
2. **Least agency** — Tools/MCP are allowlisted by default deny for in-scope production agents.  
3. **Human escalation** — Irreversible / production-impacting act classes require human approval controls, including dual control where designated.  
4. **Containment** — Budget and egress controls, with revoke procedures subject to internal objectives (target median revoke ≤ 15 minutes in drills).  
5. **Evidence** — Material allow/deny decisions are recorded for export in scoped investigations (target evidence export RTO ≤ 60 minutes in drills).  

We **do not** attest that prompt injection or model manipulation is impossible. We attest that we design for **bounded blast radius** and **provable decisions**.

Formal certification against named external standards is out of scope of this letter unless separately contracted. A control crosswalk and evidence can be reviewed under NDA.

Sincerely,  

_______________________________  
[Name], Chief Information Security Officer  
[Company]
