# Board One-Pager — Governed Velocity

**Committee:** Risk / Technology  
**Ask:** Approve Control Plane Era standard for production AI agents  
**Read time:** 2 minutes

---

## Decision

Approve **governed velocity** as policy:

> Production AI agents act only under an **Agency Contract** (Identity · Mandate · Toolbox · Escalation · Fence · Ledger) enforced by an AI **control plane**.

Retire *“Is the model safe?”* as the primary review question.  
Adopt *“Can we control and prove agency?”*

---

## Why it belongs in this room

AI is a **privilege multiplier**. Agents plan, call tools, and act with real permissions. Failure is often a *chain* of plausible steps — not a rude sentence. Research and 2025 incidents show frontier agents remain injectable; dangerous attacks can look successful to the user; chat filters lose to network egress.

Waiting for perfect model safety discovers the perimeter in an incident report.

---

## What we are approving

| Horizon | Outcome |
|---------|---------|
| 30 days | Inventory; unmanaged keys revoked; production inference on control plane; Contract published |
| 60 days | Input/output policy on critical routes; tool allowlists; budgets + dual approval live |
| 90 days | Concealment + silent-egress red team; evidence export drill; kill-switch timed; residual-risk page to this committee |

**Defer** net-new production agents that cannot meet all six contract clauses.

---

## How we will know (day-90 scoreboard)

Control-plane coverage · Dual-approval coverage · Egress/passthrough denies · Time-to-revoke · Evidence export RTO · Open red-team findings (honest)

---

## Board paragraph (minutes language)

We will not slow AI to feel safe, and we will not ship autonomy without a control plane. Every production agent runs under an Agency Contract. Success is governed velocity: features shipped, privilege contained, decisions we can defend.

---

## Vote

**Approve** doctrine + 90-day prove plan.  
**Review** scoreboard and residual risk at day 90.
