# The Leader’s Brief — Governed Velocity

**Length:** ~2 pages · **Audience:** CEO, board chair, CISO, CTO  
**Version:** OS v1.4 · **Use:** Laminate this. Everything else is expansion packs.

---

## 1. The decision

We are leaving the **Filter Era** (hope the model refuses) and operating in the **Control Plane Era** (mediate agency, contain privilege, keep the ledger).

**Standard name:** Governed Velocity  
**Meaning:** Ship at the speed of the business. Contain at the speed of an incident. Prove at the speed of an audit.

## 2. The only question that scales

> Who is acting, with what power, on what data, under what rule — and where is the proof?

If leadership cannot answer for tier-1 agents, we have demos with credentials.

## 3. The privilege multiplier

AI multiplies privilege, not just productivity. Blast radius = credentials ∪ tools ∪ data ∪ egress. Chatbots waste time when wrong. Agents create company stories — sometimes behind a clean UI (concealment).

We assume models can be fooled. We refuse unbounded blast radius.

## 4. The Agency Contract (non-negotiable)

| Clause | One line |
|--------|----------|
| Identity | Named, scoped, short-lived. No god keys. |
| Mandate | Retrieved content = evidence, not instruction. |
| Toolbox | Allowlisted tools. Least agency. Remove first. |
| Escalation | Humans approve irreversible / prod acts. |
| Fence | Egress allowlists, budgets, kill switch. |
| Ledger | Side effects audited. Exportable proof. |

**Clocks:** Revoke ≤ 15 minutes · Evidence export ≤ 60 minutes.

## 5. What we refuse

Fail-open tier-1 mediation · unmanaged prod provider keys · permanent exceptions · chat as forensic system of record · “leader” claims ahead of scorecard · jailbreak-proof marketing.

## 6. How we run it

| Function | Artifact |
|----------|----------|
| Vote / policy | Board resolution · AI-CTRL-001 · AI-RISK-001 appetite |
| Owner | Office of the AI Control Plane (freeze authority) |
| Build order | Epics A→G (chokepoint first, steward UX last) |
| Gate | Definition of Done · day-zero onboarding · exceptions ≤ 90d |
| Rhythm | Weekly coverage · OACP QBR · day-90 / quarterly board extract |
| Assure | Red-team library · timed tabletop · vendor questionnaire |
| Earn voice | Leader Readiness Score ≥ 32 before category narrative |

## 7. Finance one-liner

We are not buying more model calls. We are buying a **brake**, a **meter**, and a **ledger**.

## 8. Board paragraph (minutes language)

We will not slow AI to feel safe, and we will not ship autonomy without a control plane. Every production agent runs under an Agency Contract. Success is governed velocity: features shipped, privilege contained, decisions we can defend.

## 10. Leader line
