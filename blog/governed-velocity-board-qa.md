# Governed Velocity — Board & CISO Q&A Playbook

Use these answers verbatim or adapt. Tone: calm authority.

---

### “Won’t this slow innovation?”

Unmanaged agency is what slows innovation — after the incident, the freeze, and the forensic rebuild. Governed velocity is how we keep shipping while privilege stays bounded. The control plane is an accelerator with brakes, not a bureaucracy tax.

---

### “Can’t we rely on the model vendor’s safety layer?”

Vendor safety is necessary. It is not sufficient. It is not our identity system, our tool allowlist, our dual-approval workflow, our egress policy, or our evidence ledger. We do not outsource the right to act inside our enterprise.

---

### “Isn’t prompt injection still unsolved?”

Yes — which is precisely why Filter Era thinking fails. We assume hijack and design for containment: least agency, mediated tools, human escalation, egress fences, and a ledger. Perfect prevention is not the standard. Bounded blast radius and provable decisions are.

---

### “What’s the first dollar and the first thirty days?”

Dollar one: inventory every AI path and kill unmanaged keys.  
Days 1–30: publish the Agency Contract, force production inference through one control plane, stand up NHI hygiene. No new production agents outside that path.

---

### “How do we know it’s working?”

Not toxicity scores. Five signals: control-plane coverage, dual-approval coverage on production mutations, egress/passthrough deny rate, median time-to-revoke, evidence export RTO — plus open red-team findings we are honest about.

---

### “What if a business unit already shipped agents?”

They are not punished; they are brought under contract. Shadow AI becomes inventory, then mediation, then evidence. Exception paths expire. Permanent exceptions are how privilege multiplies quietly.

---

### “Are we behind?”

If we are still asking only whether the model is safe, we are in the Filter Era. The catch-up move is decisive: Agency Contract as policy, 90-day prove plan, day-90 scoreboard to this committee. Leaders do not narrate ambition; they show a stopwatch.

---

### “What’s the residual risk we should accept?”

We will not claim zero risk. We will claim known boundaries, tested controls, and a kill switch we have timed. Residual risk is stated in blast radius and open findings — not in reassurance language.

---

### “Why six clauses? Why not ‘just do security’?”

Because vague security becomes optional under delivery pressure. Six clauses are testable. If a production agent cannot meet Identity, Mandate, Toolbox, Escalation, Fence, and Ledger, it is not production. It is a demo with credentials.

---

### Closing line for any hostile question

We are not choosing between speed and safety. We are choosing between **governed velocity** and **unpriced privilege**. Leaders choose the first.
