# Board Resolution — Adoption of Governed Velocity

**Entity:** GuardBridge / AI control plane (this repository program)  
**Body:** Technology Committee (Program Owner consolidated)  
**Resolution No.:** 2026-08-06-AI-01  
**Date:** 2026-08-06

---

## Recitals

WHEREAS, the Company increasingly deploys artificial intelligence systems that exercise agency — including tool use, data access, and actions with operational side effects; and

WHEREAS, reliance on model safety filters alone is insufficient to govern such agency; and

WHEREAS, management has proposed **Governed Velocity** as the operating standard under which production AI agents shall operate pursuant to an **Agency Contract** enforced by an AI **control plane**;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED:

---

## Resolutions

**RESOLVED**, that the Board (or Risk Committee under delegated authority) adopts **Governed Velocity** as the Company’s operating standard for production AI agents;

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that production AI agents shall operate under the **Agency Contract** (Identity, Mandate, Toolbox, Escalation, Fence, Ledger) as set forth in Policy AI-CTRL-001, as may be amended with notice to this Committee;

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that unmanaged shared production credentials for AI providers are prohibited, and exceptions shall be time-bounded and reported;

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that management shall report to this Committee at or before day ninety (90) following adoption on:

1. Control-plane coverage for tier-1 production inference;  
2. Dual-approval coverage for scoped production mutations;  
3. Median time-to-revoke for AI credentials / entitlements;  
4. Evidence export readiness (RTO);  
5. Open material findings from agent red-team exercises; and  
6. Residual risk in plain language;

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Chief Information Security Officer is accountable for the Agency Contract standard and scorecard integrity, and the Chief Technology Officer is accountable for control-plane defaults and engineering enforcement;

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that net-new production agents that cannot meet the Agency Contract and Production Definition of Done shall be deferred absent a time-bounded, risk-accepted exception.
